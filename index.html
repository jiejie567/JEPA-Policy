01SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

The control principle

The policy learns the action
and what should happen next.

Standard behavior cloning extracts only the action. JEPA Policy also predicts the future representation produced along that same trajectory—without introducing diffusion training or sampling.

01

Paired supervision

Each demonstrated action chunk is trained with the future observation representation that actually followed it, rather than an averaged hypothetical future.

02

Shared action–future stack

Action and future tokens remain mutually visible in every Transformer layer, so future prediction directly shapes the representation that generates actions.

03

Diffusion-free deployment

A coarse joint prediction is refined once near the demonstration manifold. Two forward passes replace an iterative denoising trajectory.

JEPA-01 / SIGNAL FLOWACTION ↔ FUTURE

Shared topology

06OPERATING ENVELOPE

Scope

Strong evidence.
Bounded claims.

The simulation advantage over Diffusion Policy is positive on all eight tasks, but the mean margin is influenced by Tool Hang. The real-robot advantage over action-only is directional at ten episodes per cell.

The contribution is specific: paired future supervision inside the action-generating stack improves success with little inference overhead under the evaluated configurations.
